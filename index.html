    TIPS & TRICKS

    ＋Pay more than the minimum whenever you can. Even a small overpayment shrinks the balance interest gets charged on next month, so it compounds in your favour instead of the bank's.
    ＋Avalanche saves the most money. Snowball keeps you motivated by clearing whole cards fast. Pick whichever one you'll actually stick with for the next year or two — consistency beats optimality.
    ＋Call your card issuer and ask for a lower rate. It costs nothing, takes ten minutes, and issuers sometimes reduce APR for customers with a decent payment history, especially if you mention you're considering a balance transfer.
    ＋Stop spending on any card you're actively paying down. New purchases at 30%+ APR can undo a month of progress in a single tap.
    ＋Round your payment up. £150 becomes £160 without denting most budgets, but shaves real months off a multi-year payoff — try it in the calculator above.
    ＋Balance transfer offers can help, but read the exclusions. Some issuers won't accept transfers from certain other cards, and fees are usually 3–5% of the amount moved.
    ＋Automate at least the minimum on every card. One missed payment can trigger a penalty rate that erases months of careful progress.
    ＋If you clear a card right before its interest date, check the next statement. Some issuers apply a few days of "residual interest" even on a zero balance.
